## Macros (one central definition)

The macros are defined in the preamble of `paper/REVISED_PAPER.tex`.

| Macro | Colour (RGB) | Meaning | Contrast on white |
|---|---|---|---|
| `\revA{}` | blue (0, 72, 190) | addresses RL5B only | 7.86:1 |
| `\revB{}` | teal (0, 115, 115) | addresses Caus only | 5.68:1 |
| `\revC{}` | purple (105, 45, 160) | addresses dypE only | 8.46:1 |
| `\revShared{}` | orange (190, 85, 0) | addresses two or more reviewers; overrides the individual colours | 4.68:1 |

Supporting definitions:
- `\begin{revblock}{RevX}…\end{revblock}` colours multi-paragraph spans and `\input` files.
- `\revfloatcolor{RevX}`, placed right after `\begin{table}`, colours a whole float. LaTeX resets colour at the start of a float, so wrapping the float from outside would not work.
- All macros are `\DeclareRobustCommand`, so they are safe in captions and section titles. Section titles also use `\texorpdfstring`.

**One switch.**
- `paper/REVISED_CLEAN.tex` defines `\revclean`, which sets `\revmarkedfalse` and prints the same source in black.
- `paper/REVISED_MARKED.tex` prints it in colour.
- The two PDFs differ only in colour and in the marked build's legend paragraph at the start of the appendix (checked by `diff` of `pdftotext` output).

**Pitfall fixed.** `\color` swallows a leading space inside the macro argument. The marked build briefly read
"exhausted(in this pool". Both spans with a leading space were rewritten so the space sits outside the macro.

**Inventory.** `tools/color_span_inventory.md` lists all 115 coloured spans (file, line, colour, opening words). It is
generated by `tools/color_audit.py`. Counts: RL5B blue 7, Caus teal 44, dypE purple 24, shared orange 40. Teal and
purple counts are high because table rows and cells are tagged cell by cell.

## Overlap rule check

Every float or sentence whose issue list names two or more reviewers carries `\revShared`. Single-reviewer spans
nested inside a shared paragraph keep their own colour at the smallest coherent span. Examples:
- the pairing sentence (C) inside the orange §3.1 block;
- the detector sentence (B) after the orange Scope paragraph.

No span coloured for one reviewer serves another reviewer's issue.
