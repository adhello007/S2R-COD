## 2. Steps

| Step | What it does |
|---|---|
| **s1 assert** | Parse the ESLoss configuration out of `MyTrain.py` and assert it against the live loss object (**R5**); assert the clustered target representation is the whole image by **re-embedding** and comparing cosines (**R1**) |
| **s2 score** | Per-image ES + true error (MAE, 1−Sα, 1−IoU) for all five primary architectures, on COD10K-test and CAMO-val |
| **s3 cluster** | k swept over 9 values × 10 k-means seeds, with silhouette, seed-ARI and bootstrap-ARI curves, and a principled k chosen from them |
| **s4 correlate** | Per-cluster and per-image Spearman ρ with permutation tests and bootstrap CIs, at the principled k **and** at the old package's k=20 |
| **s5 crossarch** | Does the ES-vs-error relationship hold across architectures? |

### What is asserted rather than assumed

- **The ES convention** is *parsed from source*, not hardcoded: the `--task S2C` override block in
  `MyTrain.py`, the `PGT_Loss` construction, and `CLS.py`'s `stu.sigmoid()` / `tea.sigmoid()` call.
  A change upstream breaks the assertion instead of silently changing the measurement.
- **R1** is asserted by re-embedding target images with the full-image loader and comparing against
  the E0 cache. A cache key called `tgt` is not evidence — that conflation is how the previous
  package's grey-128 slip survived.
- **The prediction quantisation** matches `MyTest.py:76`, which writes predictions via
  `cv2.imwrite(path, cam*255)`. OpenCV **rounds**; `.astype(np.uint8)` **truncates**. The difference
  biases every prediction down ~0.5 grey levels. Verified by reproducing D2's independently measured
  endpoint MAE — see `B1_RESULTS.md` §3.4.

### How k is chosen

Not inherited. The old package fixed k ∈ {20, 50, 100} without justification. Here k is swept over
{5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 50, 75, 100, 150} with 10 k-means seeds each, and the principled k is the
**silhouette peak**. Stability (bootstrap ARI over 80 % subsamples) is measured and reported but is
**not** used to rank — it is biased toward small k, and using it selected the worst-silhouette
partition in the sweep. That discarded criterion is recorded in the log rather than quietly replaced.
Results are reported at the principled k **and** at k=20 so no conclusion rests on the choice.

## 4. Declared thresholds

Written into the script **before** running.

| # | Condition |
|---|---|
| 1 | The live `ESLoss` matches the config parsed from `MyTrain.py`, and `CLS.py` calls it on sigmoid outputs |
| 2 | **R1**: the clustered target representation is the whole image (cos ≥ 0.999 vs a fresh full-image embed) |
| 3 | Fresh inference reproduces D2's independently measured endpoint MAE within 0.001 |
| 4 | All of D2's measured leaked names are found and dropped |
| 5 | **DECLARED CLASSIFICATION**: "ES predicts the wrong objective" iff ρ(ES, 1−Sα) < 0.5 × ρ(ES, MAE) at the principled k on COD10K |
| 6 | The binary classification in (5) is stable across the principled k and k=20 |
| 7 | Every architecture falls on the same side of the 0.5 ratio boundary |

**Threshold 5 classifies; it does not gate.** Every ρ is logged with a bootstrap CI and a permutation
p regardless of which side of the boundary it falls on.

## 5. Known limits, declared

- **Per-cluster ρ depends on k and on cluster population.** Clusters with fewer than 15 endpoint
  images are excluded from cluster means. A per-cluster ρ is reported only when **≥ 5** clusters
  survive that filter — a Spearman ρ over 2 cluster means is always exactly ±1 and is not a
  measurement. CAMO-val hits that degeneracy and is reported as `DEGENERATE-NOT-REPORTED`.
- **CAMO-val is fragile at the cluster level** (250 images total) and is the checkpoint-selection set.
  Its per-image ρ is usable; its per-cluster ρ is not.
- **ES comes from one final training run per architecture.** Cross-architecture is the robustness
  substitute. **Seed-level robustness is `UNVERIFIED-DEFERRED`** because retraining is deferred.
- **True error is the teacher's error** (`Tea_epoch_best`), because that is the deployed model and what
  `MyTest.py` evaluates.
- **1−IoU is computed at a 0.5 threshold** on the min-max-normalised prediction; a soft variant is also
  stored in the per-image CSVs. Neither is from `Eval/metrics.py`, which has no IoU.
