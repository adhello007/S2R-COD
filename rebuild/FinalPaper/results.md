artifact written by a committed script. Where this file and an `EXP` block in

frozen pre-registration, which admits no p-value at n=3. The Welch intervals and TOST
p-values in §4 are **post-hoc and decide nothing**; they are reported because a reader may
fairly ask what interval the data is consistent with, and the frozen rule declines to answer.
