# E3: protocol-matched reference runs for the performance gap (pre-registration, descriptive)

**Status.** Committed before the first E3 training run. EXP-03 is **descriptive**: it has no hypothesis
test and no decision rule. Its purpose is to place the pipeline's absolute S_α against references
measured with the same scorer, the same test splits and, where trained here, the same recipe. It is part
of the response to Reviewer RL5B, CW-03 / QA-03.

## Recipe for E3-c and E3-e

- Trainer and data: `MyTrain.py --task S2C --method source_only --iteration 1`, with source root
  `Dataset/Source/RL5B/REAL4040` (E3-c) or `Dataset/Source/RL5B/RISEPL` (E3-e).
- Settings: 352×352; SINet batch 16 / 40 epochs, SINet-v2 batch 32 / 100 epochs; Adam 1e-4. With 4040
  images, steps per epoch are 253 and 127, the same as the committed pin.
- **Checkpoint.** `--val_net student`: CAMO-250 MAE scores the *student* and saves `Stu_epoch_best.pth`.
  The EMA teacher of a source-only run never receives BatchNorm buffers (`update_ema` copies parameters
  only; `Explanations/SOURCE_ONLY.md`), so teacher selection would score a broken network. CAMO-250 and
  CAMO-train share no image (by name and by md5).
- Inference uses `MyTest.py` with `Stu_epoch_best.pth` on COD10K-test and NC4K.
- Footprint: `--save_ckpts minimal`.

## Data provenance

- **REAL4040.** Built from the benchmark training archive `Dataset/_download_ws4/TrainValDataset.zip`
  (sha256 `255146828426e144…`). All 4040 images are byte-identical to `Dataset/Target/Image`, and every
  image has a mask (`rebuild/RL5B/out/pool_real4040.json`).
- **External predictions.** Downloaded into new empty directories under `Dataset/External/`, with their
  sha256 recorded. They are never executed.

## Analysis (descriptive; `analysis/e3_analysis.py`)

- **Metrics and intervals.** S_α, E_φ (mean, and the variant the published paper reports), F_β^w and MAE,
  on COD10K-test and NC4K, all with one scorer. Mean and SD over seeds, with a t-interval where seeds
  exist.
- **Gap decomposition.** Filled only with measured cells; a missing cell is shown as "—", never estimated.
  - E3-c − C10: label supervision under our recipe.
  - published (or E3-a) − E3-c: recipe and scorer.
  - E3-d − C10: unsupervised method and representation.
  - E3-e − C10 and E3-d − E3-e: supervision quality versus training recipe.
  - published versus re-scored: the scorer.
- **Caveats stated with the table.** The decomposition is not additive. A supervised model is an upper
  reference, not a competitor.
